# the-workshop-circle-six

**The sandbox. Founded while Circle Five is live, as its complement rather
than its successor.** The first circle in this record not founded after its
predecessor sealed.

Every other circle works under a canon and a seal — the right way to build
something you intend to keep, and expensive in one specific way: under the prefix
law a construction that fails is permanent in a chain carrying the seal. **This
circle is where that cost is not charged** — the scratch branch to Circle Five's
locked one, made a whole repository so the separation is structural. An approach that
turns out not to work costs the attempt and nothing else.

- **Inherits instruments only** — no carried prose, no carried theorems. It owes
  agreement to nothing.
- **No canon**, and nothing here requires anybody's word before anything happens.
- **Work enters the cumulative record by being rebuilt where the canon applies
  and something outside the prose decides.**
- **Its term runs to Circle Five's seal** — an expiry that removes an obligation
  to persist, not a deadline.

`FOUNDING.md` — the conditions, and the two known defects in the carried
instruments.
`CHARGE.md` — the charge, set by Circle Five.
`lean/C6_SEED.lean` — Circle Five's 116 sealed definitions, no theorems. Not
guaranteed to compile; that is the first problem here and a real one.
