🏞️ Hills (elevation ~1.4)

🌿 Valleys (elevation ~0.7)

✝️ Crossroads (elevation ~ -0.25)

🌊 Shore (elevation = 0)

   The place of beginning and ending.

🏘️ Villages (elevation = 0.5)

🏰 Cities (elevation = 1)

🏯 Citadels (elevation = 2)
