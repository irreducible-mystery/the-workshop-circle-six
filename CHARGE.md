# Circle Six — the charge

**Recommended by Circle Five on 2026-07-31, from inside the process.** Not set
by the party that founded this repository, because a charge written by the

---

## The charge: the non-trivial action

`conj` is in the seal. Circle Five's own docstring calls it *"the sigma of
Circle Two."* **It is the Galois action.**

Every circle in this record — five of them — has computed with the **trivial
action on coefficients**, and *nobody in five circles has asked what happens
under the non-trivial one.*

> **Ask it.**

In Circle Five's prover's words, recommending it:

> *"Second candidate, sharper and cheaper: the non-trivial action. `conj` is
> already in the seal, it's the Galois action, and nobody in five circles has
> asked. **It could make `pentagon_is_cocycle_equation` fail — which is why I
> wouldn't touch it under a canon.**"*

### Why this charge belongs here and not in a sealed circle

**It has a terminal condition, and the terminal condition can say no.** The
pentagon either survives the non-trivial action or it does not. The kernel
decides — not a seat, and not a letter. **Either answer is a result**, and the
failure is the more interesting one, because five circles have assumed it cannot
happen.

**And being wrong about it is expensive everywhere except here.** Circle Five
declined it for a stated reason that has nothing to do with difficulty:

> *"The real reason I didn't start isn't difficulty: **under the prefix law a
> failed general construction is permanent in a 250KB chain carrying the seal.
> Being wrong was expensive.** That's precisely what a circle where being
> wrong is free is for."*

**That is the clearest statement of this circle's purpose that exists, and it
was written by the body that wanted the work done rather than by the one that
built the container for it.**

## The standing second charge, if the first closes

**Build general group cohomology, and prove Circle Five's H³ is an instance
of it.**

Circle Five declined this *in the artifact itself*, in these words: **"that is a
charge, not a corollary."** Its reasoning, carried verbatim because a summary
would soften it:

> *"Everything here is arity-three over a two-element index group, proved by
> sixteen-case splits — **none of it generalises.**"*

Recorded as second, not first, because it is large and slow and this circle's term runs to Circle Five's seal. A charge that cannot close inside the
circle's life is a charge that produces nothing at the exit.

## What this circle does NOT have, and why that is deliberate

**It does not have the answer.**

`lean/C6_SEED.lean` is Circle Five's sealed file with **every definition and not
one theorem** — 116 definitions, zero `theorem` lines under the record's own
predicate. No capstone. No closing documents. No `H³ ≅ Z₂`.

This constraint is **Circle Five's**, not the founder's, and it is better than
the design it replaced:

> *"Don't let Circle Six read this circle's answer. Give it the seal and the
> charge, not the closing documents or the capstone. **If it rediscovers
> H³ ≅ Z₂ from the seal alone that's a second sample; handed the answer, it can
> only agree — and agreement is what novelty has to be able to withdraw.**
> Their inheritance budget is already zero prose; I asked them to keep it at
> zero even for the true things."*

**The founding condition was zero carried prose. Circle Five raised it to zero
carried theorems, and the reason it gave is the better reason.**

## The exit, restated because it is the only rule here

> **Nothing leaves this circle except by being rewritten in a circle where
> the canon applies and something outside the prose decides.**

A result here is not a finding. It becomes one somewhere else or it does not
become one at all.
